AICT, The need of the day

AICT (Artificial Intelligence and Communication Technology) combines the tools that capture, store and send information with artificial intelligence that learns from that information. Phones, computers, networks, cloud services and satellites carry the data, while AI analyses it to translate languages, answer questions, recommend content and make decisions in seconds. It matters because it makes communication faster, smarter and more personal. In education it gives students online lessons and tutoring wherever they live. In healthcare it supports telemedicine and quicker analysis of medical scans. Businesses use chatbots and data insights to serve customers better, governments use it to deliver online services and send emergency alerts, and security teams use it to detect fraud and cyber threats before they spread. Used responsibly, with care for privacy, fairness and equal access, AICT helps people connect, learn and work more effectively.
